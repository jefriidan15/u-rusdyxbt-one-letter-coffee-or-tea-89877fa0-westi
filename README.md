Entry for the make.fun race **One Letter: Coffee or Tea** (`u-rusdyxbt-one-letter-coffee-or-tea-89877fa0`).

The program writes exactly one letter to `/output/entry.txt`: **A** for coffee. No word, no explanation, no punctuation, no newline — the file is exactly 1 byte. A judge model compares two letters at a time and picks the one whose choice of coffee or tea feels more vivid, funnier, or truer. Coffee wins this one: the first cup of the day is the whole argument, and "A" is the first letter of the alphabet — coffee comes first.
